## Benefits of Chennai Internships

1. **Cost of Living**: Lower than Bangalore, better stipend value
2. **IT Hub**: Growing IT ecosystem with major MNCs
3. **Cultural Heritage**: Rich cultural experience while interning
4. **Transportation**: Well-connected metro and local transport
5. **Food Scene**: Excellent South Indian cuisine

## Application Tips

1. **Early Application**: Apply 2-3 weeks before deadline
2. **Skills Match**: Highlight relevant skills in resume
3. **Local Advantage**: Mention if you're from Chennai/Tamil Nadu
4. **Technical Prep**: Brush up on DSA and core CS concepts
5. **Company Research**: Study company products and recent projects
